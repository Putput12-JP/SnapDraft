What changed from the current page

  • One card treatment became three levels — hero panel is elevated glass, build rows are flat and rule-separated, charts sit on the page itself. Elevation now signals hierarchy instead of decorating every block.
  • Radius varies — 22px on the hero container, 14px inner, 11px on rows, 999px on chips.
  • All-caps mono is rationed to kickers and data labels; every heading is sentence case.
  • Asymmetric hero puts the quadrant chart in the opening viewport instead of below three screens of cards.
  • Grain overlay and a spotlight border on the hero panel — subtle texture instead of flat fills.

Why motion, here

Animation is doing analytical work, not decoration.

  • Rank changes are the finding. A silent number swap hides that Balanced topped 2024 and fell to 4th in 2025. Watching the row travel makes it unmissable.
  • Rolling digits signal which figures actually changed between two seasons.
  • Springs, not fades — travel distance reads as magnitude of change.
  • Reduced-motion is respected: transitions collapse to instant updates.

Libraries this direction uses (from pick-ui-library)

  • motion — spring physics for the board reorder, bar widths and scatter dots. Framework-agnostic build, loads straight into this no-build page.
  • NumberFlow — the pick for rolling stat digits, and not used here. Its custom element is published but is not a supported standalone API: driven directly it renders a stuck NaN and no digits. The count is tweened with motion instead. Revisit it the moment there is a React surface.
  • cmdk (⌘K palette) and recharts (charts) are React-only, so neither is used. The palette is hand-rolled on cmdk's pattern; the charts stay hand-drawn SVG. Three of the four curated picks need React — worth knowing before Vault commits to staying vanilla.
