Method

Built from completed Sleeper redraft leagues. For every team we reconstruct the draft from /draft/{id}/picks (round, slot, position — keeper picks excluded) and join it on roster_id to that season's outcome from /league/{id}/rosters and /league/{id}/winners_bracket.

Build definitions — Robust RB: 2+ RB in rounds 1–2. Elite TE: first TE in rounds 1–2. Zero RB: no RB through round 3. Hero RB: exactly one RB in rounds 1–2, none in round 3. Balanced: everything else. Archetypes are reported only at 40+ teams; lift is measured against that slice's own field baseline, so leagues with different playoff sizes stay comparable.

Why the badges exist — a ranked table of rates is mostly noise at these sample sizes. Every rate here carries a 95% Wilson confidence interval; a finding is significant only if its interval clears the field baseline entirely. It is Proven only if it is also stable — same direction in every season with enough teams. Most rows fail one of those tests, and we say so rather than ranking them anyway. In PPR 1QB only 4 of 47 openings survive the first test.

Known bias — the corpus spans 2023–2025, a stretch where early running backs beat their ADP. The RB-forward edge is real in this window but is era-dependent by construction; the by-season line on each card is there so you can see whether an edge held up or came from one season.
