When the split is wide, stop pretending there's one number

Separate bug this exposes

“BEST” is picking the worse bet, 46 times out of 46

bestOf() takes the highest price and ignores the line entirely. For an OVER, a lower line is strictly better. So on A.J. Brown we badge PrizePicks +100 at 62.5 as best, over Bet365's -110 at 57.5 — five yards easier to hit.

It's systematic, not occasional. Pick'em apps price flat by design — PrizePicks is +100/-100 on all 132 of its quotes, Underdog -112/-112 on all 45. A sportsbook's -110 can never beat +100, so a DFS app wins “best price” 100% of the time regardless of what line it's offering. The badge is a constant, not a signal.

Which is really the answer to the whole question: when a book's price never moves, the line is the only thing that distinguishes it. Showing price per book and hiding line per book is backwards. Best OVER should be the lowest line (tie-break on price); best UNDER the highest.
