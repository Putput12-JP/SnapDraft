Core draft strategies

Every team belongs to exactly one, judged on rounds 1–5.

Zero RB — no running backs at all in the first five rounds. Loads up on receivers early and hunts RB value in the middle and late rounds.0 RB in rounds 1–5
Hero RB — one anchor back, then receivers. A single high-upside RB paired with heavy WR investment.exactly 1 RB, fewer than 3 WR
Robust RB — heavy early investment at running back for depth and volume.3+ RB in rounds 1–5
WR Heavy — commits to receiver depth and upside without going full Zero RB.3+ WR and fewer than 3 RB
Balanced — a mixed start with no extreme positional concentration.exactly 2 RB, fewer than 3 WR

Positional timing

Independent overlays — a team can be Hero RB and Late QB at the same time, so these do not divide the field the way the core strategies do.

Elite QB — pays up for a quarterback early.first QB in rounds 1–4
Late QB — waits out the position entirely.first QB in round 8 or later
Elite TE — takes one of the top tight ends off the board.first TE in rounds 1–2
Late TE — punts tight end and streams it.first TE in round 8 or later

Method

Built from completed Sleeper redraft leagues. Every team's draft is rebuilt from /draft/{id}/picks (round, slot, position — keeper picks excluded) and joined on roster_id to that season's outcome from /league/{id}/rosters and /league/{id}/winners_bracket.

Build definitions — Robust RB: 2+ RB in rounds 1–2. Elite TE: first TE in rounds 1–2. Zero RB: no RB through round 3. Hero RB: exactly one RB in rounds 1–2, none in round 3. Balanced: everything else.

Metrics — Playoff Rate is the share of teams that reached the bracket. Championship is the share that won it. Top-3 Points is the share that finished top three in points scored, which measures roster strength without schedule luck. Lift is measured against each slice's own field baseline, so leagues with different playoff sizes stay comparable.

Why the badges exist — a ranked table of rates is mostly noise at these sample sizes. Every rate carries a 95% Wilson confidence interval; a finding is significant only if its interval clears the field baseline, and Proven only if it also holds the same direction in every season with enough teams.

Known bias — the corpus spans 2023–2025, a stretch where early running backs beat their ADP. Use the season filter to see whether an edge held up or came from one year.
