PART 3

Why — the cost of waiting

this year's board · Grade Keys

Best grade still available at each pick, by position. A steep line means the position empties out fast and your early picks buy a lot. A flat line means you can wait and lose almost nothing. This is the whole explanation for the table above: the builds that win buy from the steep curves and punt the flat ones.

PART 4

Your pick, priced

on the clock

At each of your turns: the best grade available now at every position, against the best you could still get at your next turn. The biggest gap is where this pick buys you the most.

Grade is a comparison tool here, not a target. It works for “which of these two backs, right now” — same position, same moment, so nothing confounds it. It does not work as a season objective: Part 2 shows the best-graded builds finishing 8th, 9th and 10th of 11. Chasing the highest team grade is how you end up as Elite TE — the best-looking roster in the league, below the field on playoff rate. Take the grade drop that buys you a scarce position.
