01 · FOUNDATIONS

Brand & logo

The shield carries the football and the Americana stripe. Wordmark is condensed athletic display caps; "FOOTBALL" rides beneath in red. Always pair the shield with clear space equal to half its height.

Primary lockup
Knockout — on dark / glass
Shield mark — app icon / FAB

DO

  • Use the shield alone as app icon, favicon, and the floating Vault Command button.
  • Keep the knockout shield on photography, glass, and dark surfaces.
  • Render "FANTASY" in red within the inline wordmark.

DON'T

  • Recolor the stripes or rotate the shield.
  • Set the full-color lockup on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Stretch, add shadows, or outline the wordmark.
